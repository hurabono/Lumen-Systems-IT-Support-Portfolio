## Business Context

Lumen Systems designs and distributes commercial packaging equipment to clients across Canada and the northeastern United States. The company relies heavily on IT for order processing, warehouse logistics systems, and remote sales team connectivity.

## Support Model

- **Tier 1 (Service Desk):** First point of contact for all tickets — password resets, account access, basic troubleshooting, hardware requests.
- **Tier 2 (Desktop Support):** Escalated hardware/software issues, on-site support, imaging.
- **Tier 3 (Systems/Network/Security):** Infrastructure-level issues, escalations from Tier 2.
